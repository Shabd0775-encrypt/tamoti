# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React + TypeScript, built to a static site and hosted on Vercel's free (Hobby) plan. No backend: daily puzzles, shared puzzles and progress all work in the browser (seeded generation, URL parameters, localStorage).

## Users

Adults and teens who want a calm break from social media feeds and a gentle workout for the mind. They play on phones and laptops about equally, in four kinds of moment:

- **Short breaks:** 2–5 minutes, such as a work break or waiting in line. The puzzle must be finishable quickly.
- **Winding down:** evenings and before bed. Must be easy on the eyes and low in stimulation.
- **Longer sessions:** 20+ minutes of focus on bigger, harder boards.
- **Daily ritual:** one puzzle a day, like a morning crossword.

## Product Purpose

A rectangle-tiling puzzle. The player picks a square grid size (5×5 to 10×10). Some cells are blocked off, and the player fills every remaining cell with a given set of squares and rectangles, dragging them from a tray and rotating them by 90°. Success is a player who leaves calmer and sharper than they arrived, and who chose this over scrolling.

## Positioning

A calm alternative to the feed, not another attention trap. Puzzles are generated by code, so every board is new and solvable, and the game can tell you how many solutions exist ("you found 1 of 3"). That rewards curiosity instead of speed.

## Operating Context

- Played with touch on phones and with mouse or trackpad on laptops. Keyboard play is supported too.
- Sessions range from 2 minutes to over 20.
- Evening play means it is often used in dim rooms.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Grid sizes from 5×5 to 10×10, with difficulty from gentle to deep.
- Interactions: drag pieces from the tray, snap to the grid, rotate by 90°, pick up placed pieces, undo, reset.
- Hints: place one correct piece, or flag a piece that can't be part of any solution.
- Solution count, plus a gallery of the solutions the player has found.
- One daily puzzle, the same for everyone. Puzzles can be shared by link.
- No accounts and no server.
- **Name:** Tamoti. Soft sounds are in, off by default.

## Brand Commitments

- **Visual direction (chosen 2026-10-30):** Japanese ink-wash (sumi-e) and watercolor with cherry blossoms, lit by alpine glow. Mostly paper and ink grays with lots of empty space, sakura pink as the one accent, a red 桜 seal as the only loud element. References in `design-reference/`.
- **Palette (binding):** the tokens in `src/styles/tokens.css`: paper, ink 900–300, mist, mist-teal, teal-deep, sakura 100–700, glow 100–300, seal-red. Only these colors and mixes of them.
- **Tone:** soothing, unhurried, quietly curious, never loud or gamified.
- **Design process:** the hero is built first and reviewed, then each remaining section one at a time. Painted illustrations (branches, landscapes) are artwork in named image slots, not drawn in CSS.

## Product Principles

1. **No pressure.** There's no visible timer by default, no fail state, no penalty for hints, and no streak guilt. Mistakes are just pieces you pick up again.
2. **A ritual, not a feed.** There's a natural place to stop, and no infinite "next" loop pulling at the player.
3. **Curiosity over speed.** Reward exploring the board, like finding another solution or understanding why a piece fits, rather than racing.
4. **Every board is fair.** Each puzzle is guaranteed solvable, and difficulty is honest.
5. **Quiet, tactile feedback.** Pieces should feel physical. Feedback is calm and never noisy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Text contrast meets WCAG AA in every theme.
- Pieces can be told apart by size and texture, not by color alone.
- The game respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Full keyboard play.
- Touch targets big enough for phones.
